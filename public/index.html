      Rulebook

      Hand ranking, best first

        Why a play is good or bad

        Each section is one idea.

        Position

        Acting last means you see what everyone else does first.

        Example: K♦ 9♦ is a reasonable open from the button.

        Number of players

        With more players, someone is more likely to hold a strong hand.

        Example: 7♣ 6♣ is playable heads-up.

        Stack depth (money)

        Your stack is your chips in big blinds (BB).

        Example: A♠ 5♠ is a shove at 10 BB.

        Opponents’ styles

        Tight players fold a lot.

        Example: the same bet means more from a passive player.

        Your hand and the board

        See what your cards make with the board.

        Example: on K♠ 7♦ 2♣ a small pair rarely wins.

        Pot odds

        Compare what you must pay with what you can win.

        What the % means

        100% is the best move. 0% is clearly wrong.
